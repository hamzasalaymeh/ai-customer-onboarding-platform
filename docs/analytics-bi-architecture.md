# Analytics & Business Intelligence Architecture Specification

Enterprise-wide analytics and business intelligence framework delivering trusted, scalable, AI-assisted analytics for data-driven decision-making across all organizational levels.

## Specification Overview

The Analytics & Business Intelligence Architecture Specification defines how the enterprise delivers analytics, reporting, dashboards, business intelligence, and advanced analytics capabilities. It covers analytics vision and framework, enterprise reporting, dashboards, KPIs, self-service analytics, data warehousing, real-time analytics, predictive analytics, and AI-driven insights.

## Specification Structure — 6 Parts

### Part 1: Analytics Foundation
Establishes the analytics vision, objectives, principles, and strategic framework. Defines the enterprise analytics foundation for delivering trusted, scalable, AI-assisted analytics across all business functions.

**Key Topics:** Analytics vision, objectives, principles, enterprise analytics framework, single source of truth, scalability, security, explainability

### Part 2: Reporting & Business Intelligence Governance
Defines enterprise reporting architecture, dashboard architecture, KPI framework, and self-service analytics. Establishes BI governance for assets, semantic models, reporting lifecycle, and enterprise standards.

**Key Topics:** Reporting architecture, dashboards, KPI framework, self-service analytics, BI governance, data certification, reporting standards

### Part 3: Advanced Analytics & Visualization
Establishes data visualization standards, semantic layer, OLAP models, predictive analytics, and AI-driven analytics. Defines how enterprises leverage advanced analytics and AI for competitive advantage.

**Key Topics:** Data visualization, semantic layer, OLAP models, predictive analytics, AI-driven analytics, forecasting, automation

### Part 4: Data Platform
Defines data warehousing architecture, data marts, real-time analytics, streaming analytics, and embedded analytics. Covers the data infrastructure supporting analytics and BI across the enterprise.

**Key Topics:** Data warehouse, data marts, real-time analytics, streaming analytics, embedded analytics, ETL/ELT, dimensional modeling

### Part 5: Governance & Enterprise Readiness
Establishes governance policies, KPI management, audit procedures, and enterprise readiness assessment. Ensures analytics and BI framework is effectively implemented, monitored, and continuously improved.

**Key Topics:** Analytics governance, KPI management, analytics audit, compliance monitoring, enterprise readiness, reporting standards

### Part 6: Enterprise Implementation Guidelines
Provides phased implementation roadmap and maturity assessment for establishing enterprise analytics and business intelligence. Covers implementation phases, analytics maturity model, review checklist, documentation references, and continuous optimization.

**Key Topics:** Implementation phases, analytics maturity model, review checklist, production readiness, continuous improvement, enterprise readiness
