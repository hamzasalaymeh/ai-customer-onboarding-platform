# Enterprise Identity & Access Management (IAM) Architecture Specification

Enterprise-wide identity and access management framework delivering secure, scalable, compliant identity lifecycle management, authentication, and authorization across all organizational systems and applications.

## Specification Overview

The Enterprise Identity & Access Management (IAM) Architecture Specification defines how the enterprise manages digital identities, authenticates users, authorizes access, and maintains compliance across all systems. It covers identity lifecycle management, authentication services, authorization frameworks, governance, and enterprise readiness.

## Specification Structure — 6 Parts

### Part 1: IAM Foundation
Establishes the IAM vision, objectives, principles, and strategic framework. Defines the enterprise identity foundation for delivering secure, scalable, compliant identity and access management across all organizational systems.

**Key Topics:** IAM vision, objectives, principles, enterprise IAM framework, identity governance, security, compliance

### Part 2: Identity Lifecycle & Authentication
Defines identity lifecycle management, identity provisioning, modification, deprovisioning, synchronization, and authentication architecture. Establishes authentication services for identity verification and adaptive authentication.

**Key Topics:** Identity lifecycle, provisioning, deprovisioning, synchronization, authentication architecture, passwordless authentication, adaptive authentication

### Part 3: Privileged Access Management
Covers privileged access management (PAM) for managing elevated access to critical systems. Establishes privileged identity management, credential vaults, just-in-time access, and session recording for secure privileged access.

**Key Topics:** PAM, privileged identity management, credential vault, just-in-time access, session recording, privileged session monitoring, privileged access governance

### Part 4: Identity Federation & Integration
Defines identity federation, trust relationships, and federation protocols (SAML, OAuth 2.0, OIDC). Establishes federation gateways for seamless cross-domain identity management and integration with external systems.

**Key Topics:** Identity federation, trust relationships, SAML 2.0, OAuth 2.0, OIDC, federation gateway, cross-domain authentication, federated access

### Part 5: Advanced Authentication & Authorization
Establishes advanced authentication services including SSO, MFA, adaptive authentication, and risk-based authentication. Defines fine-grained authorization frameworks for complex access scenarios.

**Key Topics:** SSO, MFA, adaptive authentication, passwordless, risk-based authentication, RBAC, ABAC, authorization engine, access decisions

### Part 6: IAM Governance & Enterprise Readiness
Provides comprehensive IAM governance framework, compliance management, identity risk management, and enterprise implementation guidelines. Covers identity compliance, auditing, monitoring, and maturity assessment.

**Key Topics:** IAM governance, identity compliance, regulatory framework, identity auditing, identity monitoring, identity risk management, PAM governance, secrets management, external identity, enterprise readiness
